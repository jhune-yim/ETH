# From Exact Counting to Circuit Lower Bounds

*A Reconstruction of the Announced Refutation of ETH and Its Verification Obligations*

An expository paper centered on Corollary 1.4 of OpenAI's announced refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis (ETH). Conditional on the reported SAT bounds, it reconstructs an infinitely-often superlinear circuit lower bound for a language in $\mathrm{E}^{\mathrm{N}}$, with explicit reduction costs and verification obligations.

latexmk -pdflua -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error ETH.tex
